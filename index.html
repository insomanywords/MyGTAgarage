About My GTA Garage v1.60.5

My GTA Garage is the ultimate tool for GTA Online car collectors to track, organize, and manage their vehicle fleets.

1. Vehicle Management

  • Add Vehicle: Click the + Add button on the main screen. Select the Make and Model. Choose the Garage and Floor. Set the slot number, wheels, plates, and toggle Weaponized or Favorite status. You can optionally add financial data including the LSCM Price (Vehicle Value) and Sell Price.
  • Edit Vehicle: Click the icon on any vehicle card to modify its details, update its location, change its custom image, or edit notes and financial values.
  • Custom Images: When adding or editing a vehicle, use the "Vehicle Image" section to upload a picture from your device. If you are signed in to Google Drive, the image will be securely uploaded to your character's Drive folder (with a real-time progress bar) and linked automatically.
  • Copy/Duplicate: Open a vehicle's detail view (by clicking on its card) and click Copy. This opens the Add window with all the car's details and custom image pre-filled, leaving only the slot number blank for rapid entry of duplicate cars.
  • Move Vehicle: Inside the edit or detail view, click Move. Select the destination garage. The app will automatically find the first empty floor and slot. If the destination is completely full, you will be prompted to select a vehicle to swap places with.
  • Sell Vehicle: Click Sell in the edit or detail view to simulate selling a car. This removes the car from your garage, logs the transaction (along with the vehicle's Sell Price) in your Sales History, and starts an 18-hour real-time cooldown timer.
  • Share Vehicle: Open a vehicle's detail view and click Share. The app will generate a clean snapshot image of the vehicle card that you can instantly send via text, Discord, or save to your device to show off to potential LS Car Meet buyers.

2. Organization & Search

  • Grouping: Use the dropdown menu at the top to group your vehicles by Garage, Brand, Class, Drive type, Wheels, Plates, or Weaponized status. Groups can be collapsed or expanded by clicking their headers.
  • Sorting: When viewing by "Group: Garage", vehicles are automatically sorted by their Slot number (1, 2, 3, etc.). Vehicles without a slot number are placed at the end in the order they were added.
  • Search: Use the search bar to instantly filter your collection by vehicle name, manufacturer, location, or notes. Garage capacities will dynamically update to show the real total usage even while filtering.
  • Favorites: Mark your best cars by toggling the Favorite checkbox when adding/editing. Select "Favorites Only" from the view dropdown to display only these vehicles.

3. Garage Tools

  • Floor Nicknames: In the Add/Edit window, click the "Edit Nickname" button next to the Floor selector to assign a custom name to that specific garage floor (e.g., "JDM Floor", "Supercars"). These nicknames will appear on the group headers in the main view.
  • Swap Entire Garages: Click the button next to the view dropdown to instantly swap the entire contents of two different garages.
  • Capacity Tracking: The app actively tracks your garage capacities. It hides floors that are full in the Add menu, warns you if a garage has no space left, and shows real-time usage (e.g., 5/10) on every floor header.

4. Data & Cloud Sync

  • Character Management: Manage multiple, completely isolated vehicle collections per character via the Settings menu. You can add, rename, or delete characters. Renaming a character locally will automatically rename their corresponding Cloud folder.
  • Google Drive Sync: Sign in via the "Google Drive" tab in Settings. The app automatically creates a "My GTA Garage" folder and auto-syncs your database to the cloud after every change. Custom car images are also stored securely in your Drive.
  • Offline Mode & Safety: The app saves everything locally to your browser first. If you lose internet, your data remains safe and will sync the next time you connect. The app checks for conflicts when signing in on a new device to prevent accidental overwrites, prompting you to choose between Local or Cloud data if there is an actual data conflict.
  • Sessions & Timeouts: For security, Google strictly limits client-side web application sessions to exactly 1 hour. Once your token expires, the app will gracefully show a "Session Expired" alert and the cloud status icon in the header will turn gray. Simply click the cloud icon to sign back in.
  • Manual Cloud Backups: Use the "Create Manual Cloud Backup" button in Settings to save a timestamped snapshot of your garage to Google Drive, ensuring you always have a fallback.
  • Local Backups: Prefer local files? Use the "Local Data" tab in Settings to download your garage as a JSON file to your device, or load an existing JSON file.
  • Cloud Deletion: If you delete a character or Factory Reset the app from the Danger Zone, you will be prompted with the option to also completely delete your associated Cloud Backup folders from Google Drive to save space.
