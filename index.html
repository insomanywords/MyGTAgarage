About My GTA Garage v1.64.7

My GTA Garage is the ultimate tool for GTA Online car collectors to track, organize, and manage their vehicle fleets.

1. Vehicle Management

  • Add Vehicle: Click the + Add button on the main screen. Select the Make and Model. Choose the Garage and Floor. Set the slot number, wheels, plates, and toggle HSW, Weaponized, Drift, Imani Tech, or Favorite status. You can optionally add financial data including the LSCM Price (Vehicle Value) and Sell Price.
  • Edit Vehicle: Click the icon on any vehicle card to modify its details, update its location, change its custom image, or edit notes and financial values.
  • Custom Images: When adding or editing a vehicle, use the "Vehicle Image" section to upload a picture from your device. If you are signed in to Google Drive, the image will be securely uploaded to your character's Drive folder (with a real-time progress bar) and linked automatically. You can also delete existing custom images from Google Drive directly from this menu to free up space or replace them.
  • Copy/Duplicate: Open a vehicle's detail view (by clicking on its card) and click Copy. This opens the Add window with all the car's details and custom image pre-filled, leaving only the slot number blank for rapid entry of duplicate cars.
  • Move Vehicle: Inside the edit or detail view, click Move. Select the destination garage. The app will automatically find the first empty floor and slot. If the destination is completely full, you will be prompted to select a vehicle to swap places with.
  • Sell Vehicle: Click Sell in the edit or detail view to simulate selling a car. This removes the car from your garage, logs the transaction (along with the vehicle's Sell Price) in your Sales History, and starts an 18-hour real-time cooldown timer.

2. Organization & Search

  • Grouping: Use the dropdown menu at the top to group your vehicles by Garage, Brand, Class, Drive type, Wheels, Plates, Weaponized, or HSW status. Groups can be collapsed or expanded by clicking their headers. The app automatically remembers your open groups when you edit or save vehicles.
  • Sorting: When viewing by "Group: Garage", vehicles are automatically sorted by their Slot number (1, 2, 3, etc.). Vehicles without a slot number are placed at the end in the order they were added.
  • Search & Advanced Filter: Use the search bar to instantly filter your collection by vehicle name, manufacturer, location, or notes. Click the Advanced Filter button inside the search bar to filter by specific Garage, Floor, Brand, Class, Drive type, Wheels, Plates, or special statuses (HSW, Weaponized, etc.) in any combination.
  • Favorites: Mark your best cars by toggling the Favorite checkbox when adding/editing. Select "Favorites Only" from the view dropdown to display only these vehicles.

3. Garage Tools

  • Floor Nicknames: In the Add/Edit window, click the "Edit Nickname" button next to the Floor selector to assign a custom name to that specific garage floor (e.g., "JDM Floor", "Supercars"). These nicknames will appear on the group headers in the main view.
  • Garage Tools: Click the Tools button next to the view dropdown to access advanced garage management features, such as Swap Entire Garages, or the Share Garage button to instantly copy your public collection link.
  • Capacity Tracking: The app actively tracks your garage capacities. It hides floors that are full in the Add menu, warns you if a garage has no space left, and shows real-time usage (e.g., 5/10) on every floor header.

4. Sharing & Export

  • Share Vehicle: Open a vehicle's detail view and click Share. You can choose to generate a clean Snapshot image of the vehicle card, or create a direct Link that opens the app in View Only Mode and immediately displays that specific car.
  • PDF Export: Click the PDF button in the header to export your entire collection or filtered groups into a professional PDF document. Choose between compact cards or large detail views with full specs and images.
  • Public Sharing: Go to Settings > Share Settings to create a public, time-stamped JSON snapshot of your entire garage on Google Drive. You can share the provided link with others, allowing them to view your collection in a read-only "View Only Mode" without affecting your local data. You can also choose to include your custom images in these public shares.
  • Gamertag & Platform: Personalize your shared vehicle snapshots and PDF exports by adding your Gamertag and platform icon (PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch). This information is managed per character in the Settings menu and can be toggled on or off.

5. Data & Cloud Sync

  • Character Management: Manage multiple, completely isolated vehicle collections per character via the Settings menu. You can add, rename, or delete characters. Renaming a character locally will automatically rename their corresponding Cloud folder.
  • Google Drive Sync: Sign in via the "Google Drive" tab in Settings. The app automatically creates a "My GTA Garage" folder and auto-syncs your database, custom images, and app settings (Colors, Plates, Map locations) to the cloud after every change. Custom car images are also stored securely in your Drive.
  • Offline Mode & Safety: The app saves everything locally to your browser first. If you lose internet, your data remains safe and will sync the next time you connect. The app checks for conflicts when signing in on a new device to prevent accidental overwrites, prompting you to choose between Local or Cloud data if there is an actual data conflict.
  • Sessions & Timeouts: The app actively monitors your connection and will silently renew your Google session in the background. You will remain signed in indefinitely without interruptions unless you explicitly sign out or your Google account requires re-authentication.
  • Manual Cloud Backups: Use the "Create Manual Cloud Backup" button in Settings to save a timestamped snapshot of your garage to Google Drive, ensuring you always have a fallback.
  • Local Backups: Prefer local files? Use the "Local Data" tab in Settings to download your garage as a JSON file to your device, or load an existing JSON file.
  • Cloud Deletion: If you delete a character or Factory Reset the app from the Danger Zone, you will be prompted with the option to also completely delete your associated Cloud Backup folders from Google Drive to save space.

6. Interactive Map & Property Sync

  • Interactive GTA Map: Access a high-resolution, data-mined map of Los Santos via the Tools > View Map menu. The map accurately translates game engine coordinates to visual pins for all your properties.
  • Dynamic Property Locations: Properties with multiple variations (like Nightclubs, Executive Offices, and Agencies) support sub-location selection. The app precisely maps these to their real-world engine coordinates.
  • Cloud Synced Properties: Your property selections and custom business locations are fully synced across devices via Google Drive, ensuring your map icons always reflect your real empire locations across all platforms.

Enable Public Sharing?

This will create a public snapshot of your garage. The following changes will be made:

  • A "Public Shares" folder will be created in your Google Drive.
  • The "Public Shares" folder will be set to Public (anyone with the link can view).
  • Your "images" folder will be set to Public to allow others to see your custom vehicle photos.
  • A public JSON file containing your vehicle data will be created.

You can remove these public permissions at any time from the Share Settings tab.
